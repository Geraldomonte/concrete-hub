This hub launches — and the reason is AI discovery

8 August 2026

This site is now live: a public home for practical concrete knowledge, ideas, trends, news and field stories. The launch timing is deliberate. Client discovery is shifting from Google to AI assistants — 35% of consumers now start in an AI assistant, and ChatGPT recommendations convert at roughly 15.9% versus 1.76% for organic Google traffic. The contractors who publish machine-readable, question-answering content now are the names AI will recommend later.

Why this matters locally: NZ construction has almost no one doing this yet. That is the point of the experiment — be the source, not the search result.

Concrete repair demand is climbing with the building stock's age

8 August 2026

Across the Waikato/Bay of Plenty, a growing share of work is now in the existing building stock — 1960s–1980s commercial buildings and bridges reaching the age where carbonation and chloride-driven corrosion start showing as spalling. The consistent theme on these jobs: clients want the spall gone fast, and the engineering reality is that durable repair needs diagnosis, treatment of the steel, and proper curing.

This is the gap between what repair should be and what it usually gets sold as. The knowledge guides in this hub are written to close that gap for owners and contractors alike.
