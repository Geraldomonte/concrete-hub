Render notes. Shading = per-face linear gradients (light from above: top lightest, front mid, side dark). Materials = feTurbulence mottle filters (conc, concSoft, soilF, woodF) over the gradient fills, plus light aggregate stipple at low opacity. Depth = radial contact shadows under elements, ambient darkening at face junctions, crisp edge lines (Revit "shaded with edges" presentation). Rust-red stays reserved for corrosion, strands, sealant, inserts and annotation — never a large fill. Wood #c9a26f, soil #a8926f, concrete #cfc9bf and steel #9aa3ab are proposed tokens: if approved they become a DESIGN.md §2.1 addition before site code uses them. All figures remain inline SVG — no images, no external requests, crawlable, scale to any width.
