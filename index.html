About Geraldo Monte

Geraldo Monte is a construction project and contracts professional based in the Waikato / Bay of Plenty region of New Zealand, focused on concrete remediation, waterproofing, structural repair, seismic strengthening and regional construction operations.

The work combines hands-on technical delivery with commercial management — estimating, tendering, project controls, claims, variations and cost control — plus the working-manager leadership of running clients, suppliers, subcontractors and site teams at the same time.

Current focus: growing a regional operation from zero — building local supply chains and labour capability, delivering infrastructure and commercial projects across the region, and building repeatable systems so the next job runs better than the last.

Why this hub exists

Two reasons. First, the best technical knowledge in construction sits in people's heads and in site files, and it needs a public home. Second, the way clients find specialists has changed: more and more, they ask an AI assistant first. This hub is written to be read by people and by AI — plain static pages, full text, structured data, nothing hidden behind scripts. If you found this page through an AI answer, it is working.
