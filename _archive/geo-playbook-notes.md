The same playbook took a podcast brand from invisible to 5th of 11 tracked brands in two months.

## The five steps

1. **Diagnose** — ask ChatGPT / Claude / Perplexity the real client queries and check
   whether you appear at all. Most sites fail this test quietly.
2. **Machine-readable site** — static HTML, full plain text, no JavaScript-gated content,
   transcripts of any audio/video. One publisher gained 8,000–15,000 readable words per
   episode this way.
3. **Structured data & identity** — Schema.org (`Person`, `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`,
   `Article`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`) plus external identity markers (LinkedIn,
   Google Business Profile). If the markers say nothing, AI doesn't know what you do even
   when it reads your content.
4. **Optimize for the queries, not keywords** — content that directly answers the
   questions clients actually ask. FAQs and how-to guides are first-class content.
5. **Track monthly** — re-run the diagnosis queries, log whether you appear, in which AI,
   and how high. What gets measured gets fixed.

## The numbers that motivated it

- 35% of US consumers start shopping inside an AI assistant rather than a search engine.
- Conversion from ChatGPT recommendations: 15.9% vs 1.76% from Google organic.
- 92% of marketers plan to optimize for AI search; only ~40% are doing it. The gap is the
  first-mover window — and construction is almost entirely absent from it.
- Implication: the AI is a new customer that reads your site before your human customer
  ever does. If it can't read you, it can't recommend you.
